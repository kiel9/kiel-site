## What it does
It turns raw meeting notes or a transcript into structured minutes. It pulls out the decisions and action items, and drafts the tasks and tickets that should follow from them.

## Input
Raw notes or a transcript from a client or team meeting.

## Output
Minutes published to the team wiki, a list of decisions and action items, and draft tasks and tickets. It proposes them; nothing is created until I approve it.

## Before and after
Before, I wrote the minutes by hand after the meeting and copied each action item into the tracker myself.

Now I review one draft and approve the follow-ups in a single pass.
