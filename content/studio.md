## What we do
I set up your client or partner integration for you, from the first spec to go-live. I also build AI agents for the repetitive work between commercial and ops teams. That means reconciliation, status chasing, and re-keying data that already exists somewhere else.

## Who it's for
Founders of small B2B companies in Indonesia who need to connect with a client or partner and have no integration team of their own.

I work in Bahasa Indonesia and English.

## How an engagement works
We start with a call about the integration: who you connect to, what moves between the systems, and the go-live date.

I read the partner's specification against itself and send a written review of blockers, inconsistencies and open questions. Both sides sign off on one shared spec and the scope.

I build the integration and test it with you and the partner. Three days before testing, a go-live gate checks configuration, deployment and test data, so problems are fixed before the meeting.

Go-live, then handover of the signed-off spec to your team. I stay with you while the first live traffic arrives.
