# About

I'm Jehezhiel Marlon Djamari. Most people call me Marlon.

I spent eight years as a software engineer. Desktop and mobile programming first, then ERP and banking systems as a developer and consultant, then two years as a full-stack engineer at an Indonesian health platform. I studied Computer Science at Binus and finished in 2013.

In 2021 I moved into product at that same health platform. The switch wasn't a rejection of engineering; it was realising the most expensive bugs I'd shipped were requirement bugs, not code bugs. Since then I've owned product in logistics commercial operations, on a data marketplace and analytics platform, and on life insurance policy servicing — automating reinstatement, renewal and corporate services.

Today I'm a Senior Product Manager at a health claims administrator in Jakarta. I own partner integrations: wiring an insurer into our systems so policies, members and claims move without anyone re-keying them. I take a partner from the first commercial conversation through specification, UAT and go-live, then stay with them once real claims start arriving.

The title says product. The work is mostly translation — API contracts into commercial terms, sales promises into scoped work, a partner's technical document into tickets an engineer can build from without asking me a question.

Four things I try to do consistently. I check the data before I answer, and I'm willing to come back and say the partner was right. I look for the blocker that is actually holding a project, not the stated one. I turn incidents into gates that run before every launch, so a problem solved once stays solved. And I read partner specs adversarially — a contradiction caught on the page is far cheaper than one caught in UAT.

Outside the day job I'm building AI automation for Indonesian B2B businesses, and writing down what I learn.

I work in Bahasa Indonesia and English. I'm based in Jakarta. Reach me on LinkedIn or by email.
